Sunday, no reason, went through the shelf. Every single book I have actually finished was bought while I was avoiding something.

Not a crisis. An ordinary bad hour. A bus I didn't want to get on. A call I wasn't returning. An afternoon where going into a shop was easier than going back to the flat.

The ones I buy on good days are for a woman with a free evening and a settled head, and I have never met her. I keep buying for her anyway. It's either hope or a filing error.

> Eleven unread books on the middle shelf, every one bought in a good mood. I keep them as evidence.

The ones I finish were bought as an exit. Which is bleak, and also the most accurate thing I've worked out about myself this year, and I worked it out from a shelf.
