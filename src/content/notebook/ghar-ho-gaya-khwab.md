```verse
Ghar jaana bhi ab ek khwab sa lagta hai,
Jabse ek khwab ke liye ghar chhoda hai.
Jo khwab palkon pe tha, woh sheher le aaya,
Jo apna tha, usi apne se door kar aaya.
```

```verse
Socha tha, manzil milegi toh sukoon hoga,
Kya khabar thi, sukoon bhi ek junoon hoga.
Yahan khwab poore hote hain, neendein kam hoti hain,
Har jeet ke baad kuchh khaali si shaamein hoti hain.
```

```verse
Jis aasmaan ko chhoone nikli thi ghar se,
Us aasmaan mein bhi ghar dikhta hai aksar nazar se.
Ek khwab tha—kuchh banne ka, kuchh paane ka,
Ek khwab tha—apnon ke sar fakhr se uthane ka.
```

```verse
Magar waqt ne bhi kaisi baazi kheli,
Manzil mili, toh ghar ki yaad aur gehri nikli.
Ab lautne ki tamanna bhi khwab si rehti hai,
Maa ki awaaz sun loon toh aankh nam si rehti hai,
Papa poochhte hain, "sab theek hai?" toh baat tham si jaati hai.
```

```verse
Aur jo ghar kabhi bas ek jagah hua karta tha,
Wahi ab dil ka sabse haseen khwab hua karta hai.
Kya ajeeb sa hisaab hai zindagi ke safar ka—
Ek khwab paane nikli thi,
aur khwab ho gaya ghar ka.
```
