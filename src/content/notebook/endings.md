Every few months everyone loses it because a book ended badly, and every few months it emerges that the book ended honestly.

An ending isn't a reward for finishing. You did not put in four hundred pages of emotional labour and earn a wedding like a stamp card at a coffee place.

> A sad ending you didn't want and a bad ending are not the same thing, and we have completely stopped telling them apart.

I've loved books that flattened me. I've resented books that gave me exactly what I asked for, and I have never once reread one of those, which should tell you something about what I actually want.

And look — the real reason I'm annoyed. I have had a full year of endings I did not ask for and nobody is letting me leave a review of those.
