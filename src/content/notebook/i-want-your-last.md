```verse
I know I'm not the easiest to hold,
my temper quick, my patience cold,
I overthink, I feel too fast,
make small things heavy, built to last.

but loving you is never hard
you make it simple, safe, unscarred,
where I see storms, you make it clear,
the world feels softer when you're near.

I know sometimes I overreact,
turn moments sharp, distort the fact,
but if it takes me changing too
I'll learn, I'll grow, I'll meet you.

not losing me, just choosing right,
to love you better, softer, light.

and like Klaus Mikaelson said to Caroline Forbes
“he's your first love, I intend to be your last,”

I don't want fleeting, don't want past,
I don't want almost, don't want fast
I need you steady, need you vast,
I don't want first,
I want your last.
```
