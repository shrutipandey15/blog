Important isn't a genre. It's what people say when they've stopped reading and started arranging themselves.

I did it for four years. Every time I said it I told you nothing about the book and everything about who I wanted you to think I was, which is a horrible thing to see written down but there it is.

> Nobody has ever stayed up till four in the morning for a book that was merely important.

Tell me it wrecked a weekend. Tell me you were angry at it in a queue at the bank. Tell me it made you briefly nicer to your mother and then it wore off. That's a recommendation. Important is a CV.
