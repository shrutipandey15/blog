People say it like they're doing you a favour. You'll understand it when you're older. As though understanding is a queue and I stood in the wrong one.

I understood it. At fourteen, in my body and not my vocabulary, which is a real kind of understanding and honestly the more expensive one.

> Some books aren't early. They're early to a room you haven't been let into yet.

Reread it last month. Found nothing new. That was the point. Nothing in the book changed. I finally had somewhere to put it.

Also everyone who said that to me at fourteen should know I'm still thinking about it, which I'm aware is not normal.
