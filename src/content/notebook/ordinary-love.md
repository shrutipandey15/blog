```verse
I do not want a love that only speaks
in poems, in stars, in promises deep;
I want the kind that quietly stays
through ordinary, unremarkable days.
```

```verse
The kind that knows when I've had enough,
and holds me close when the world gets rough.
That hears "I'm fine" and knows it's a lie,
and stays beside me without asking why.
```

```verse
I want your hand when we're crossing the street,
your tired old smile when our eyes finally meet;
your voice asking, "Did you eat today?"
and stealing my fries in the usual way.
```

```verse
I want the mornings when neither speaks,
the sleepy hellos, the messy weeks;
the little arguments over something dumb,
then laughing at how ridiculous we've become.
```

```verse
I want to know how your face will change,
when years leave their marks, familiar and strange.
To watch your hair slowly turn into grey,
and still look at you the very same way.
```

```verse
Because people don't live in forever,
we live in moments stitched together—
in cups of tea and late-night calls,
in waiting outside, in missed trains and falls.
```

```verse
So if you ask what loving you means,
it isn't the grandest of human dreams.
It's choosing you when the day isn't new,
when life is mundane, and we're both worn through.
```

```verse
And if someday my life reaches its end,
I won't wish for more time to spend.
I'll only be grateful, through all that we knew,
that an ordinary life became extraordinary
because it was lived with you.
```
