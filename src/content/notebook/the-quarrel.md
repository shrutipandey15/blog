```verse
There is a quarrel in me
that begins before I wake
one voice gathering reasons to leave,
another finding reasons to stay.
```

```verse
By morning, they have worn me thin;
by night, they have changed their names.
One calls it fear,
the other calls it wisdom,
and both arrive carrying the same old flame.
```

```verse
I have said yes with my mouth
while something in me whispered no.
I have stood at the edge of beautiful things
and wondered how long
before they go.
```

```verse
There are doors I have opened
with my hands shaking inside,
and doors I have walked away from
just to prove
I could survive outside.
```

```verse
What a strange thing
to be both the wound
and the hand that presses it,
to want to be held
and still flinch
when someone gets close enough to do it.
```

```verse
Some nights, I choose myself.
Some nights, I choose the fear.
Most nights, I cannot tell
which one is speaking
when I hear.
```

```verse
So I let them speak.
The house grows quiet.
The argument thins to a whisper.
Outside, morning gathers
at the edge of the window
and for once,
I do not pull the curtains.
```
